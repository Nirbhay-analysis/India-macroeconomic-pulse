## 📌 Executive Summary & Analytical Insights
* **2012–2016 Disinflation Regime:** Headline CPI cooled sharply from 10.2% to 4.5% during an economic expansion phase (>8% GDP).
* **2020 Pandemic Shock:** GDP contracted by -6.6% due to COVID-19 lockdowns, while inflation remained sticky due to supply chain disruptions.
* **2024 Core Normalization:** Core CPI softened to a 12-year low of 3.5%, isolating price volatility primarily within food and fuel components.
